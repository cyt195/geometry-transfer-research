# AI-assisted work guide

AI assistance is permitted for debugging, test generation, code generation, symbolic or algebraic exploration, plotting, code review, and documentation. It is not authoritative evidence that a theorem is true.

## Required human-auditable record

For material AI-assisted output, retain:

- the question and assumptions supplied to the tool;
- the generated code or derivation in version control;
- deterministic tests and seeds where applicable;
- an independent check of constants, signs, and edge cases;
- an accurate label such as exploratory, post-hoc, numerical, or confirmatory.

## Prohibited shortcuts

- Do not accept a generated proof without line-by-line audit.
- Do not weaken failing tests, suppress counterexamples, or tune metrics to desired results.
- Do not upload private or frozen artifacts to an external model unless explicitly authorized under the applicable data policy.
- Do not let generated prose invent citations, empirical values, preregistration status, or historical metric definitions.
- Do not reconstruct missing historical actions or labels using a new law.

AI can help find a counterexample; it cannot decide that the counterexample is inconvenient and should be discarded.
